# ethicalAI in the Wild
## Playbook for Ethical AI in the Wild with an example in Uber's Transition to Autonomy

### Stakeholder Analysis
1. Scoping 5 key stakeholders as Uber begins its transition to 100,000 autonomous cabs beginning 2027
    
    Stakeholders: Current Uber Drivers, City Governments, Local Businesses, Regulators, Other road-users
2. Actionable, acceptable, and just transition pathways for affected stakeholders. Program motivates common minimum value creation between Uber and affected stakeholder.

### Forthcoming: 
A reproducible playbook for any platform based service provider moving to autonomy using artificial intelligence. This policy playbook can be used to identify key governance and regulatory requirements as well as foster a culture of care and caution. The playbook helps measure and mitigate the impact of the organization's move to autonomy and outlines actionable pathways for stakeholder buy-in.
